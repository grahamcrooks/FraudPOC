🕰️ Background
Bupa has traditionally caught fraud after a claim is paid, through audits and recoveries. This proof of concept tests whether the same detection can happen before payment, using AI to review receipts and patterns at scale.
🗂️ What the POC Will Entail
Extract and analyse approximately 10,000 historical claims receipts, replaying them initially through Phase 1, with future phases to follow.
⚙️ How the POC Will Work
01Extract — AI extracts text and structured data from every receipt
02Analyse — checked against configured rules based on signals
03Classify — scored and grouped by risk level
04Route — clean claims pass; flagged claims go to an investigator
05Validate — suspicious claims validated by the fraud team

One Problem. Three Layers of Defence.
🔍 Receipt Forensics
Is this receipt real and valid?
Pre-flight — one AI read, then business rules at no AI cost
  • Receipt type and field extraction with per-field confidence
  • Disqualifying content — quotations, proformas, voids, non-medical
  • Receipt completeness — paid in full, ABN, itemised, signed
  • Claim value against the high-value threshold
Forensic analysis — runs only on receipts that clear pre-flight
  • Claim-to-receipt match
  • Font consistency — spliced typefaces
  • Colour and stamp analysis — digital overlays
  • AI-generated receipt detection
  • Metadata and authoring trail
  • Duplicate detection — fingerprinted against every prior claim
⚡ Cross-Claim Signals
Does this claim make sense?
  • ES-001 Distance anomaly — lodged far from the member's registered address
  • ES-002 Device ring — unrelated members submitting from one device
  • ES-003 Bank account ring — unrelated practices paying into one account
Further strategies — phantom ABN, waiver abuse, item code validation — are planned and not in this build.
🧠 Network Intelligence
Who else is involved?
  • Network graph traversal — every entity a claim touches, up to 3 hops: member, practice, practitioner, device, submission IP, payment account
  • Fraud community mapping — links a claim to fraud already confirmed, not just to itself
  • Retrospective reach — an alert covers every claim attached to the entity, including claims already assessed and closed
  • Invisible to conventional systems — no single claim contains the connection
Fraud case similarity matching is planned. It needs a corpus of confirmed cases, which this system produces as it runs.
